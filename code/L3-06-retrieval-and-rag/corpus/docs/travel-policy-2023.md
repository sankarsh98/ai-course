# Travel and Expenses Policy (2023)

Effective: 1 March 2023. Status: superseded. Owner: Finance.

## Booking travel

Flights and trains should be booked through the travel desk. Economy class is required for all flights regardless of length. Book as early as you can.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap is $180 per night, including taxes, in all cities. Short-term rentals are not allowed.

## Meals and incidentals

Travellers receive a daily meal allowance of $55 for each day away from home. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Ground transport and mileage

Use of your own car for business is paid at 0.38 dollars per kilometre. Parking and tolls need receipts.

## Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims in the Ledgerly portal no later than 60 days after the trip ends. A receipt is required for any single expense above $40. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run after approval.

## Approval

Your line manager approves claims up to $1,500.
