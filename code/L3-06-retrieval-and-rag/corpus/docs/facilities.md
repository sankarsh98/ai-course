# Office and Facilities Guide

Effective: 1 March 2025. Status: current. Owner: Facilities.

## Opening hours and access

The Leeds office is open from 7:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. Your badge opens the front door and the floors you work on. Out-of-hours access needs approval from your manager and a note in the facilities log. If you lose your badge, report it at reception; a replacement badge costs $20 and is ready the same day.

## Parking

The office car park has 40 spaces, allocated by monthly permit. Apply for a permit through the facilities request form by the 20th of the previous month. Eight spaces have electric vehicle chargers; charging sessions are limited to 4 hours so that others can use them. Bicycle storage and showers are on the ground floor.

## Visitors

Register visitors in the visitor system at least 24 hours before they arrive. Visitors must be accompanied at all times beyond reception and must not enter laboratories without safety induction.

## Meeting rooms

Book meeting rooms in the calendar. Rooms not checked in within 15 minutes of the start time are released automatically.

## Reporting a problem

Report broken equipment, leaks or heating problems through the facilities request form. Anything urgent or dangerous should be reported to reception in person.
