# Customer Support Service Levels

Effective: 1 April 2025. Status: current. Owner: Customer operations.

## Priority levels

Every support ticket gets a priority when it is opened.

- P1, critical: a customer's whole monitoring network is down or data is being lost. First response within 1 hour, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- P2, high: one site or several loggers are affected. First response within 4 business hours.
- P3, normal: questions, single logger problems and feature requests. First response within 2 business days.

A first response means a person has read the ticket and replied; it does not mean the problem is fixed.

## Escalation

If a P1 ticket is not resolved within 8 hours, it is escalated automatically to the duty engineering manager. Customers can ask for escalation of any ticket at any time by replying with the word ESCALATE in the subject line.

## Channels

Customers can open tickets through the support portal, by email, or by phone. Phone support is staffed from 8:00 to 18:00 UK time on business days; outside those hours only P1 calls are answered by the on-call engineer.

## Closing tickets

A ticket is closed when the customer confirms the fix, or automatically after 7 days without a reply from the customer.
