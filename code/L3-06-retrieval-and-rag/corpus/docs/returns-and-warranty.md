# Returns and Warranty

Effective: 1 January 2025. Status: current. Owner: Customer operations.

## Warranty cover

All Brindlewood loggers carry a 3-year warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers faults in materials and workmanship. It does not cover damage from lightning, animals, incorrect installation, or opening the sealed housing. Water damage is covered only if the housing seal was intact and the cable connectors were closed.

## Getting a repair

Before sending anything back, ask customer operations for a return authorisation number (RMA). Parcels without an RMA number on the label are refused at the repair centre in Leeds. Repairs under warranty are free, including shipping both ways. Out-of-warranty repairs are quoted before work starts.

## Returning an unwanted product

Customers may return an unwanted product within 30 days of delivery for a refund. Unopened products are refunded in full. Opened products are refunded minus a 15 percent restocking fee. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days of the return arriving.

## Replacements on site

For customers on the Field Plus service plan, a replacement logger is shipped before the faulty one is returned, so monitoring can continue. The faulty unit must then reach the repair centre within 21 days, or the replacement is invoiced.
