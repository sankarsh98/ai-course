# Travel and Expenses Policy (2025)

Effective: 1 January 2025. Status: current. Owner: Finance.

## Booking travel

All flights and trains must be booked through the company travel desk so that the trip appears on the duty-of-care map. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. For flights of six hours or longer, premium economy may be booked with written approval from your director. Book at least 14 days ahead where possible; late bookings cost the company on average a third more.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap is $210 per night, including taxes, in most cities. In high-cost cities (London, Zurich, New York, Tokyo and Singapore) the cap rises to $260 per night. If a conference hotel is more expensive than the cap, you may stay there when the event organizer requires it; attach the event page to your claim. Airbnb and other short-term rentals are allowed only for stays of five nights or more.

## Meals and incidentals

Instead of collecting receipts for food, travellers receive a daily meal allowance of $65 for each full day away from home. On the first and last day of a trip the allowance is 75 percent of the full rate. Alcohol is never reimbursed, even with a client.

## Ground transport and mileage

Taxis and ride-hailing are fine between the airport and the hotel and for late-night travel. Use of your own car for business is paid at 0.45 dollars per kilometre, which covers fuel, insurance and wear. Parking and tolls are claimed separately with receipts.

## Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims in the Ledgerly portal no later than 45 days after the trip ends. Claims submitted after that window need a written exception from Finance. A receipt is required for any single expense above $25; a photo of the receipt is enough. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run after approval.

## Approval

Your line manager approves claims up to $2,000. Claims above $2,000 also need approval from the budget holder of the project the trip was charged to.
