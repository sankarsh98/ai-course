# Data Retention Policy

Effective: 1 May 2025. Status: current. Owner: Legal.

## Purpose

This policy sets how long Brindlewood keeps different kinds of data, so that data needed for customers and the law is kept, and everything else is deleted on time.

## Customer sensor readings

Sensor readings uploaded by customers to the Brindle Cloud service are kept for 7 years after upload, unless the customer deletes them earlier or the contract says otherwise. After the contract ends, readings are kept for 90 days so the customer can export them, then deleted.

## Support records

Support tickets and call recordings are kept for 3 years after the ticket is closed.

## Employee records

Personnel files are kept for 6 years after employment ends. Unsuccessful job applications are deleted 12 months after the hiring decision.

## Backups

Backups of production systems are kept for 35 days. Data deleted from the live system therefore disappears from backups within 35 days.

## Deletion requests

Requests from individuals to delete their personal data must be completed within 30 days of receipt. Forward any such request to the legal team the same day you receive it.
