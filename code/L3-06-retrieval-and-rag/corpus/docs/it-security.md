# IT Security Standard

Effective: 1 June 2024. Status: current. Owner: Security team.

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords for company accounts must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of several unrelated words is easier to remember than a short complex password. You do not need to change your password on a schedule; change it only if you think it has been exposed. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and every system that holds customer data. Use the authenticator app; text-message codes are accepted only as a temporary fallback.

## Phishing

If an email asks for your password, payment details or an urgent transfer, do not reply and do not click links. Use the Report Phish button in the mail client, which sends the message to the security team and removes it from your inbox. If you already clicked a link or typed your password, call the service desk straight away so the account can be locked.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the service desk within 1 hour of noticing, on extension 4400 or from any phone on +44 113 496 0400. The device will be wiped remotely. Reporting fast matters more than knowing exactly where it was lost.

## Laptops and storage

Company laptops use full-disk encryption, which must never be turned off. USB storage devices are blocked by default; if you need to move large files, use the shared drive or request a temporary exception. Install software only from the company software centre.

## Working away from the office

Do not use public computers for company work. On public Wi-Fi always connect the VPN first. Lock your screen whenever you step away, even at home.
