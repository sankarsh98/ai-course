# New Starter Onboarding

Effective: 1 January 2025. Status: current. Owner: People team.

## Before your first day

You will receive an email with your start time and the name of your onboarding buddy, a colleague from another team who answers everyday questions during your first month. Please send a passport-style photo for your badge at least three days before you start.

## Your first day

Collect your laptop and badge from reception at 9:30. The service desk will help you sign in and set up multi-factor authentication. Your manager will go through your first 90 days plan with you.

## Mandatory training

Every new starter must complete the Safe Lab Practice course within 10 working days of starting, before entering any laboratory. Information security and data protection training must be completed within your first month. Training is assigned automatically in the learning portal.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. Your manager holds review meetings at the end of months one, three and five. During probation the notice period on either side is two weeks.

## Benefits enrolment

Enrol in the pension and health insurance schemes within 30 days of your start date. If you miss the window, you can only join at the next annual enrolment in November.
