# BW-40 Soil Moisture Logger: User Guide

Effective: 1 February 2025. Status: current. Owner: Product team.

## What the BW-40 does

The BW-40 is a field logger that measures volumetric soil moisture and soil temperature at up to four depths. It stores readings internally and sends them over Bluetooth to the Brindle Field app, or over LoRa radio when the optional radio module is fitted.

## Power and battery life

The BW-40 runs on two AA lithium cells. At the default 15-minute logging interval, a fresh pair lasts about 18 months. Shorter intervals and frequent Bluetooth downloads shorten battery life. Alkaline cells are not recommended because they fail in cold weather.

## Environmental limits

The housing is rated IP67, so it survives temporary immersion in water up to 1 metre. The logger operates from -30 to 60 degrees Celsius. Do not leave the cable connectors open in rain.

## Memory

Internal memory holds 2 million readings. When memory is full the logger stops recording unless overwrite mode is turned on in the app, in which case the oldest readings are replaced first.

## Status light

The status light blinks green once every 10 seconds during normal operation. Two red blinks mean the battery is low and should be replaced within two weeks. A steady red light means a sensor fault; check the cable and see the firmware notes for the error code shown in the app.

## Bluetooth and the app

Bluetooth range is about 30 metres in open ground and less in crops or near buildings. Pair the logger by holding the magnet key against the logo for three seconds.

## Calibration

The sensors should be recalibrated every 12 months, or after any repair. Calibration is done at the repair centre in Leeds.
