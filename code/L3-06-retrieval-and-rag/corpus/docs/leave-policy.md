# Leave Policy

Effective: 1 January 2024. Status: current. Owner: People team.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount based on contracted hours. Leave builds up monthly, so in your first year you can take only the days you have earned so far, unless your manager agrees to an advance.

## Carrying leave over

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March; after that date they expire and are not paid out. Days above the carry-over limit are lost at the end of the year, so plan holidays early.

## Requesting time off

Request leave in the Rota system. For absences longer than three working days, give at least 14 calendar days of notice. Your manager should answer within five working days; a request with no answer after that time is not automatically approved, so chase it.

## Sickness

If you are ill, tell your manager before your normal start time. You can self-certify for up to 3 consecutive working days. From the fourth day of sickness a doctor's note is required. Sick days do not reduce your annual leave.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 20 weeks of parental leave at full pay, followed by up to 12 further weeks at half pay. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks at full pay, which can be taken at any time in the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Other leave

Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for a close family member. Jury service and military reserve duty are paid at full salary. Volunteering leave allows 2 paid days per year for registered charities.
